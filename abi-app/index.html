Percent Black, all counties

Personal integrity violations combine violence and organised hostility (lynchings, race riots, intimidation, the Klan, sundown towns, hate groups and similar); civil liberties violations combine legal and institutional measures (enslavement, executions, incarceration, police killings, redlining, school segregation, state laws and similar). Values are rescaled so that, on each dimension, the lowest county or state mean in any decade is 0 and the highest is 100; higher means more anti-Black, and intervals can run below 0 or above 100. Colours are seven quantile classes, pooled over all decades (“All decades”) or taken from the selected decade alone (“This decade”). Bands show 95% posterior intervals. Ctrl-, ⌘- or Shift-click a second unit (or use “Compare”) to add it to the charts. Counties use 2010 boundaries and appear from the decade they entered the data; hollow points mark county-decades with no county-level data, whose values rest on the state’s.
