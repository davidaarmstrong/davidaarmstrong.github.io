Hostility combines violence and organised hostility (lynchings, race riots, intimidation, the Klan, sundown towns, hate groups and similar); treatment combines legal and institutional measures (enslavement, executions, incarceration, police killings, redlining, school segregation, state laws and similar). Values are on the model’s latent scale: higher means more anti-Black. Colours are seven quantile classes, pooled over all decades unless “Colour within decade” is ticked. Bands show 95% posterior intervals. Counties use 2010 boundaries and appear from the decade they entered the data; hollow points mark county-decades with no county-level data, whose values rest on the state’s.
